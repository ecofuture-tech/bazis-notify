# bazis-notify 0.1.0

The first release. Requires Bazis 2.11 and bazis-users 2.9; live delivery needs bazis-ws
2.6 (the extra `ws`).

- `bazis.contrib.notify.service.notify(users, type, title, body, ...)` stores a
  `Notification` per recipient with the texts in his language, publishes it after the
  commit over bazis-ws and, with `email=True`, sends it through the mail backend of the
  core; `notify_all` for many users, `delete_expired` for the retention
  (`BAZIS_NOTIFY_RETENTION_DAYS`).
- The types are registered by the apps in `<app>/notifications.py`
  (`bazis.contrib.notify.registry.register`).
- `bazis.contrib.notify.router`: the user lists, reads and deletes his own notifications.
  The routes are `UserRequiredRouteBase` routes of bazis-users: a request without a valid
  token is 401, as on the other routes that require a user (not 403).
